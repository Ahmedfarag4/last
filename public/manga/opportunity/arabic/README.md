# Opportunity Manga — Arabic PDF Edition

This folder is for the user-provided Arabic illustrated Opportunity manga, **below the English chapters** in Mission Archives → Opportunity → Manga Archive.

Upload four PDFs using the exact filenames:

- `chapter-01.pdf` — 8 pages (global pages 1–8)
- `chapter-02.pdf` — 13 pages (global pages 9–21)
- `chapter-03.pdf` — 2 pages (global pages 22–23)
- `chapter-04.pdf` — 17 pages (global pages 24–40)

The supplied Arabic chapter 3 had printed pages 23 and 22 in reverse order, so the upload package corrects their order.

Download `opportunity_arabic_chapters_for_github.zip` from the project handoff, unzip, and upload the four `.pdf` files to this folder. Do not upload the zip as one file.

The PDF reader is shared with the existing English version. Reading progress for Arabic is stored separately. English PDFs and manga archives of InSight/Apollo are unchanged.
